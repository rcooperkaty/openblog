Roundabouts were designed to make traffic flow smoothly. They were not designed as launching pads for sedans trying to ignore the laws of geometry.

## The Featured Fail
We’ve all seen it: someone decides that the circular lane is just an optional decoration rather than a path to follow. Watching a car take a "shortcut" directly over the center landscaping is the kind of chaotic energy we crave.

## Why It’s Funny
It’s the confidence. The driver isn't panicking; they are fully committed to the wrong decision. Watching someone maintain 30mph while driving *over* a pile of mulch and bushes is the definition of "confidently incorrect."
