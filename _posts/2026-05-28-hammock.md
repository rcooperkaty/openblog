Nature is beautiful, peaceful, and apparently, out to get you. 

Today we're talking about the [hammock on a dead tree phenomenon](https://www.reddit.com/r/Whatcouldgowrong/comments/1tan7gc/what_went_wrong_with_her/). You find the perfect spot, string up your hammock, and think you’ve reached peak relaxation. You haven't. You've reached peak potential for a spectacular, gravity-based epiphany.

There is no sound quite like the snap-crash of a rotten branch, followed by the sight of someone plummeting to the forest floor. It’s the universe’s way of reminding us that no matter how hard we try to chill, it’s still in charge.
