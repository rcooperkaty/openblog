Nature is beautiful, peaceful, and apparently, out to get you. 

## The Featured Fail
Today we're talking about the "hammock on a dead tree" phenomenon. You find the perfect spot, you string up your hammock, and you think you’ve reached peak relaxation. You haven't. You've reached peak potential for a spectacular gravity-based epiphany.

## Why It’s Funny
There is no sound quite as specific as the *snap-crash* of a rotten branch, followed by the sight of someone plummeting to the forest floor. It’s the perfect reminder that no matter how hard we try to relax, the universe will occasionally find a way to remind us who is actually in charge.
