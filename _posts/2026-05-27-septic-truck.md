We've all been in a hurry. You're late, the light is red, and you start calculating the risks. But most of us draw the line at "trying to outrun a train in a septic truck."

## The Featured Fail
This absolute classic from [r/Whatcouldgowrong](https://www.reddit.com/r/Whatcouldgowrong/comments/1t7szqw/wcgw_popping_a_truck_tire/) involves a driver who clearly decided that a freight train was a gentle suggestion rather than a massive, unstoppable wall of steel. Spoiler alert: The train won.

## Why It’s Funny
It’s the sheer absurdity of the vehicle choice. If you’re going to engage in high-stakes railroad chicken, maybe don't do it in something that carries a literal cargo of liquid regret. The physics are unforgiving, but the comedy is gold.
