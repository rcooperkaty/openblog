We've all been in a hurry, but most of us have the sense to draw the line at "trying to outrun a freight train in a septic truck." 

This classic [WhatCouldGoWrong post](https://www.reddit.com/r/Whatcouldgowrong/comments/1tan7gc/what_went_wrong_with_her/) features a driver who clearly decided that a literal wall of steel was just a gentle suggestion. Spoiler alert: the train didn't care.

If you're going to engage in high-stakes railroad chicken, maybe don't do it in a vehicle carrying a cargo of liquid regret. The physics are unforgiving, but the comedy is absolutely gold.
