Roundabouts were designed to make traffic flow smoothly. They were not, however, designed as launching pads for sedans trying to ignore the laws of geometry.

We’ve all seen it: someone decides that the [circular lane](https://www.reddit.com/r/Whatcouldgowrong/comments/1tan7gc/what_went_wrong_with_her/) is an optional decoration. Watching a car take a "shortcut" directly over the center landscaping is the kind of chaotic energy we crave.

The best part isn't even the crash; it's the confidence. The driver isn't panicking—they are fully committed to the wrong decision. Maintaining 30mph while driving over a pile of mulch and bushes is the literal definition of confidently incorrect.
